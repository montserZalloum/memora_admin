<objective>
Change `StageResult.time_spent` from seconds to milliseconds and remove the legacy `POST /progress/complete` endpoint. The session-based `POST /sessions/end` is the canonical completion path.

Purpose: The legacy `/progress/complete` endpoint duplicates logic from `/sessions/end` and bypasses session validation. Removing it simplifies the codebase and ensures all completions go through the session pipeline. The `time_spent` unit change aligns with client-side millisecond timestamps.

Output: Updated `StageResult` model, removed legacy endpoint and its helper functions/imports, deprecated models.
</objective>
