   c. Phone normalization: `if self.mobile: self.mobile = self._normalize_mobile(self.mobile)`
   d. Mobile mandatory for new docs: `if self.is_new() and not self.mobile: frappe.throw("Mobile number is required for new players", frappe.ValidationError)`

6. **`on_update(self)`:** Call `self._hash_password()` only. This fires on every save but the `if self.__new_password:` guard inside `_hash_password` prevents unnecessary work.
